Virtual Eating Disorder Recovery Program

Eating disorders can have a profound and far-reaching impact on every aspect of your life. These complex mental illnesses affect not only your physical health but also your emotional well-being, relationships, and daily functioning. From intrusive thoughts about food and body image to compulsive behaviors that disrupt your routines, eating disorders can leave you feeling trapped in a cycle of anxiety, shame, and isolation. The toll on your self-esteem, confidence, and overall quality of life can be devastating.

This virtual recovery program is designed to help you break free from these destructive patterns and reclaim your life. Using evidence-based treatment modalities, innovative interventions rooted in neuroscience, and personalized coaching, we offer a comprehensive approach to healing. My program addresses the physical, psychological, and social aspects of eating disorders, providing you with the tools and support needed to transform your relationship with food, your body, and yourself. Whether you're struggling with anorexia, bulimia, binge eating disorder, ARFID or other forms of disordered eating, this program will guide you on your journey to recovery and help you build a healthier, more fulfilling life.

What You'll Receive:

  • Initial 90-minute Assessment: We'll map out your recovery goals and create a personalized treatment plan.
  • 18 Sessions (50-minute virtual sessions): 3 per month for 6 months (program can be modified based on treatment needs).
  • Digital Resource Library: Access a wealth of educational materials, worksheets, and recovery tools.
  • Personalized Action Plans: Receive tailored assignments to reinforce your progress between sessions.
  • Email Support: Reach out to your therapist for brief guidance as needed.
