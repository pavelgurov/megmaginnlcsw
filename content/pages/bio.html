Meg Maginn, L.C.S.W

Meg Maginn, LCSW, is a distinguished psychotherapist with over 30 years of experience in mental health treatment. As the founder and director of Eating Disorder Associates and Lifeworks NY, she has dedicated her professional career to providing comprehensive, compassionate care for individuals, groups, couples, and families.

Her expertise is in Eating Disorders and complex trauma and spans to multiple critical areas, including addiction, ADHD, OCD and other mental health challenges. Meg also works with LGBTQIA+ and transgender populations, offering a nuanced and inclusive approach to mental health care. She is trained in diverse therapeutic modalities and holds post graduate certificates in EMDR, Neuro Linguistic Programming (NLP), Ketamine/psychedelic assisted therapy and evidence-based treatments like CBT, DBT, Imago couples therapy, divorce mediation and internal family systems.

Beyond her clinical practice, Meg serves as a life coach, clinical supervisor, public speaker, workshop/retreat leader, writer and professional motivational consultant. She works with mental health practitioners seeking to develop private practices, and is notably one of the first practitioners licensed to conduct psilocybin-assisted therapy in Oregon. Currently, she is involved in research on the impact of Ketamine and psilocybin on eating disorders and mental health.

Meg’s innovative approach emphasizes personalized, evidence-based treatment through collaborative efforts with medical professionals, schools, and community-based programs, ensuring comprehensive and holistic mental health care.
